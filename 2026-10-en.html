Dear colleagues,

Call it sustainability, future-proofing or simply good business. Ultimately, it comes down to the same question: how do you keep your business strong, healthy and relevant for the long term?

In conversations across DFG companies, I see that every company faces its own challenges. Yet the same themes keep coming up: legislation, rising costs, customer expectations and the availability of people. That means there is a lot we can learn from each other.

During the Sustainability Ambassador Day, we saw a strong parallel with nature: resilient ecosystems depend on strong connections. The same applies within Dutch Flower Group. Our strength lies in working together, sharing knowledge and building on each other’s experiences. Our taskforces are one of the ways we do this. From October onwards, the DFG Sustainability Council will help us maintain focus and make progress together.

In this newsletter, you will find several practical examples: from lessons we can learn from nature and the impact of Fairtrade to steps towards a traceable wild-harvesting supply chain. Different topics with one thing in common: by working together, we can learn faster and make a greater impact.
Linda van Waveren
Group Sustainability Manager
Dutch Flower Group
